About Skeyy Architecture

Skeyy is a modern eCommerce platform designed to help vendors launch and grow their online stores with ease. Unlike traditional marketplaces, Skeyy offers lower commission percentages, making it more profitable for sellers to run their business online.

Our platform is simple, user-friendly, and built for efficiency. Vendors can easily add, update, edit, or delete products, manage orders, direct messages, stock, inventory, returns, and exchanges—all from a single, powerful dashboard.

Skeyy stands out by rewarding early growth and partnerships. When a vendor joins Skeyy and refers other vendors to the platform, they unlock special benefits, including commission percentage rewards. This referral advantage applies to the first nine vendors who join through them, giving early partners an exclusive opportunity to earn more while helping the community grow.
